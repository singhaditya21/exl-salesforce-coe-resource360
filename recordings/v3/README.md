# Resource 360 complete narrated master suite v3.0

Masters 03–11 record the 89 remaining unique governed Salesforce screens. Master 12 composes the executive account-to-completion golden path and ends with the synchronized GitHub Pages companion. Each authored configuration generates a branded coverage slide, narration, captions, a 1080p H.264/AAC master, poster, timeline, contact sheet and QA report.

Raw captures are intentionally ignored. They are produced through authenticated Salesforce browser interaction at the fixed recording viewport and may be regenerated after `pnpm sf:prepare-demo`.

Build one master with `node recordings/v2.1/build-live-master.mjs recordings/v3/master-03.config.mjs`, or the complete remaining suite with `node recordings/v3/build-suite.mjs`.
